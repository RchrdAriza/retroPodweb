WHAT IS RETROPOD?

The iPod experience,
on your device.

RetroPod is a local music player built with Flutter, designed to bring back the iconic iPod Classic experience. Navigate your personal music library through a faithful virtual interface — Click Wheel, Cover Flow, Now Playing screen and all — without depending on a single streaming service.

Your collection. Your playlists. Your hardware. The way it was meant to be.
